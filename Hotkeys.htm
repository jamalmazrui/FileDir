This is the complete list of FileDir commands, given three ways so you can find a command however you happen to be thinking about it. Press Control+F1 to turn on Key Describer, which says a command’s name, key, and description instead of running it.

  • Shift+P – Previous Tagged: or Control+UpArrow, Go to previous tagged item

  • Shift+S – Say Size: Say the size of the current item in a form you can take in at once, such as 1.5 megabytes
