Other Programs FileDir Uses

FileDir calls a number of separate programs and ships some of them. Each keeps its own licence, which is not changed by being used here:

  • 2htm, by the same author, MIT License. Ships with FileDir.
  • 7-Zip, by Igor Pavlov, GNU LGPL with an unRAR restriction. Ships with FileDir.
  • SharpZipLib, MIT License. Ships with FileDir.
  • Tektosyne, by Christoph Nahr, MIT License. Ships with FileDir.
  • Ude, a port of the Mozilla universal charset detector, Mozilla Public License. Ships with FileDir.
  • Pandoc, by John MacFarlane, GNU GPL version 2 or later. Installed separately, machine wide, and called as a program.
  • ExifTool, by Phil Harvey, Perl Artistic License or GNU GPL. Installed separately and called as a program.
  • ffmpeg, GNU LGPL or GPL depending on the build. Installed separately and called as a program.
  • ImageMagick, under the ImageMagick License, which is derived from Apache 2.0. Installed separately and called as a program.
  • yt-dlp, in the public domain under the Unlicense. Installed separately and called as a program.
  • Ollama and the language models it runs, each under its own terms. Installed separately and asked over a local web interface.

Calling a program is not linking to it, so none of these terms reach FileDir’s own source. The four installed separately are not redistributed by FileDir at all: the installer offers to fetch them from their own publishers.
